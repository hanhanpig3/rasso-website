Positioning Formula

Golden Ratio Placement

The launcher top position uses the golden ratio (φ ≈ 0.618) relative to the available work area. On 1080p display (workArea 1032px): y = 267px. Ensures visual weight sits slightly below center, matching human gaze patterns.
